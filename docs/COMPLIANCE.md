# Compliance and Data Governance

FounderSignal must treat compliance as a product capability, not a footer.

## Requirements
- Use licensed providers and legitimately accessible public information.
- Store provenance for production records.
- Minimize protected data in search indexes and logs.
- Provide privacy access/removal workflows.
- Maintain suppression so removed data is not blindly re-imported.
- Apply purpose limitation and configurable retention.
- Encrypt data in transit and at rest.
- Maintain tenant isolation and audit events.
- Apply rate limiting and scraping/export abuse controls.
- Never claim an unverifiable accuracy percentage.
- Clearly distinguish inferred fields from observed/verified fields.

## AI
LLMs must not invent contact data. AI-generated classifications/summaries should cite or reference source evidence when used for scoring or user-facing recommendations.
